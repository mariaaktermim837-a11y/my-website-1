🌍 My Travel Dreams

The world is a book and those who do not travel read only one page. Here are the places I dream of visiting!

My Bucket List Destinations

Traveling is one of my greatest passions. I believe exploring the world — its cultures, food, and people — is one of the most enriching experiences a person can have.


Below are the four places I dream about the most. Each one is unique and magical in its own way!

🗼 Paris

France

The City of Love! Paris has always been magical to me — the Eiffel Tower, charming cafés and world-class art museums.

  • Visit the Eiffel Tower at sunset
  • Explore the Louvre Museum
  • Try fresh croissants and macarons

🏯 Tokyo

Japan

A breathtaking blend of ancient tradition and futuristic technology. I love Japanese culture, from cherry blossoms to neon lights!

  • See cherry blossoms in the park
  • Visit Senso-ji Temple
  • Try authentic ramen and sushi

🏛️ Rome

Italy

Rome is a living museum — every corner holds thousands of years of history. Plus the food is amazing!

  • Explore the Colosseum
  • Toss a coin in Trevi Fountain
  • Eat fresh pasta and gelato!

🎡 London

United Kingdom

London is a vibrant, historic city. From Buckingham Palace to the West End, there is so much to see and experience!

  • See Big Ben and Westminster
  • Walk through Hyde Park
  • Enjoy English afternoon tea
